About Us

We are travellers first, and writers second.

Our story

Wanderlust started in 2019 as a shared Google Doc between three friends trying to plan a trip through Southeast Asia. It quickly became apparent that the internet had no shortage of travel advice — but plenty of it was outdated, sponsored, or just plain wrong.

We built Wanderlust to fix that. Every recommendation on this site comes from real visits, honest opinions, and zero paid placements. Our team has collectively visited over 60 countries, and we update our guides whenever we go back — or when the world changes around them.

What we cover

We focus on three categories: beaches (from hidden coves to famous shores), temples (ancient ruins to living sacred spaces), and countries (full destination guides with culture, logistics, and itinerary ideas).
